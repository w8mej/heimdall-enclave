### Continuous Differential Conformance Fuzzing Bridge & Anti-Drift Defense

Static mathematical models and mechanized proofs guarantee logical soundness within their formal axiomatic domain, but they do **not** inherently guarantee that the production Rust implementation does not suffer from **semantic drift** over time as code is refactored, optimized, or extended.

To close this verification-implementation gap and detect semantic drift dynamically, Heimdall-Enclave couples the **94-module / 1,036-theorem** Lean 4 suite with a unified 5-layer verification gate and **9-target** continuous differential property fuzzing:

* **Unified Anti-Drift Gate:** [`scripts/verify-formal-bridge.sh`](../../scripts/verify-formal-bridge.sh)
* **Harness & Runner:** [`crates/enclave-agent-runtime/tests/formal_conformance_fuzz.rs`](../../crates/enclave-agent-runtime/tests/formal_conformance_fuzz.rs) & [`scripts/run-fuzz.sh`](../../scripts/run-fuzz.sh) (`./scripts/run-fuzz.sh --iterations=5000`)
* **9 Differential & Adversarial Boundary Targets:**
  1. **FlowGate FSM Bisimulation (`RustModelBridge.lean:Theorem 11`):** Asserts live Rust `FlowGate::on` transitions strictly commute with `lean_spec_step` or fail-closed into `QuarantineAbort`.
  2. **Dual Security Lattice Monotonicity (`Lattice.lean`):** Property-fuzzes preorder laws, Bell-LaPadula no-write-down, and Biba integrity absorption ($T_3 \sqcup T_i = T_3$).
  3. **QIF Exact Integer Pricing Non-Undercharge (`QifBound.lean:Theorem 2` / `FractionalQif.lean`):** Fuzzes `millibits_for_outcomes(k)` against arbitrary outcome spaces to verify non-undercharge.
  4. **Context DAG Provenance Taint Immunity (`ContextDagProvenance.lean:Theorem 23`):** Injects adversarial $T_3$ tool returns into multi-turn DAG nodes, verifying monotonic integrity tracking.
  5. **RFC 8785 Canonical JSON Invariants:** Fuzzes arbitrary JSON payloads to assert serialization idempotency and key sorting before cryptographic signing.
  6. **Rolling 24h QIF Window Ledger (`RollingQif.lean`):** Fuzzes hostile backward clock jumps, multi-entity charges, and `L1`/`L2`/`L3` caps to verify clock-rollback immunity, cap bounds, and all-or-nothing atomicity.
  7. **Delegation Slice Conservation (`DelegationAccounting.lean:Theorem 35`):** Fuzzes negative slices, negative budgets, and `i64::MAX` boundaries to verify strict additive conservation and signed-overflow immunity.
  8. **Capability Heap Isolation (`CapabilityProtocol.lean` / `HeapAccounting.lean`):** Fuzzes live ingest, derive, release, and wipe sequences to verify strictly monotonic `literal_exposure`, mandatory $T_3$ tool tagging, and immediate post-release/wipe unresolvability.
  9. **Adversarial Wire-Format & Parser Boundary:** Throws arbitrary mutated bytes and UTF-8 strings at `jcs::canonicalize_str`, `UserTurnV1`, `QifLedger` (`TryFrom` validation), and `pii::scan`, asserting zero panics and fail-closed rejection.
* **Audit Artifacts:** Outputs continuous test metrics and shrink logs to [`reports/fuzz-report.md`](../../reports/fuzz-report.md) and [`reports/fuzz-report.json`](../../reports/fuzz-report.json).

`ExtractionGapDefense` is a handwritten model record, not extracted Rust/MIR/LLVM. No extraction pipeline is present. Cryptographic, OS, memory, scheduling, channel, hardware and external policy inputs remain assumptions of the relevant models. Other declarations are model specifications even when a historical name or comment suggests a system guarantee. Changes to theorem statements require explicit regeneration and review of `theorem-types.json`; CI never regenerates it automatically.

F144 restatement: the data-dependent structural language has an executable checker that rejects T3 reads in targets and grammar arguments; the noninterference theorem derives its safety judgement from this check. The finite charging model enumerates complete transcripts from power-of-two alphabets and proves their count from whole-bit charges. A deterministic natural-weight channel computes joint/prior maxima from actual rows and derives the needed inequalities. The later `NormalizedQif`, `AdaptiveQif` and `FractionalQif` modules add normalized rational kernels, arbitrary-depth observation-dependent strategies and exact fractional budget reasoning; the fuller adaptive/rolling/delegated/fallible results and remaining objectives are recorded in row 14 of [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md). The pinned continuation gate actually compiled these modules. Their existence does not establish clock/SEP behavior, production interpreter/caller refinement, physical observation equivalence or two-attestor composition. Those remain concrete implementation/correspondence gaps, not missing mathematical models or hidden hypotheses claiming system security.
